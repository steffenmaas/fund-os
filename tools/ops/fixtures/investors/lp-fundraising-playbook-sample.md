## Scoring: Fit

Seven dimensions, raw maximum 113.

### Dimension 1 — Fund investor (0–20)

| Signal | Points |
|---|---|
| Core business is fund commitments | 20 |
| Unclear | 6 |

### Dimension 2 — First-fund openness (0–20)

Open to first funds: 20. Asks for a track record: 2.

It also counts whether the investor has signed at least one first fund in the last three years; a single hint is not enough, it takes a documented example with date and fund name from a public or the fund's own source.

## Scoring: Timing

Four dimensions, sum 100. Valid for 60 days.

| Dimension | Points |
|---|---|
| Current commitments | 0–35 |
| Allocation window / fund cycle | 0–25 |

A window counts only when a statement of the investor or a dated source supports it; without a date the statement is no evidence and the window counts as closed until a newer source says otherwise.
