# Operations screens

Seven single-file HTML pages that give the partners one place to start the day, see the pipeline, approve what the autopilot proposes, publish content, and read the fund's own documents. They are published as **Claude artifacts** and call the viewer's own connectors (Attio, Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar) under the viewer's own login. Nothing here holds a secret, and nothing here names a fund: every fund-specific value sits in one `const CONFIG = { ... }` block at the top of each page's script.

They are the front end of the autopilot layer (`ops-*` skills, `tools/ops/`): the autopilot proposes, these screens are where a person approves, undoes and reads the feed.

| File | Screen | What it does | Connectors it calls |
|---|---|---|---|
| `start.html` | Start | The entry page: one tile per screen (Deal Cockpit, Investor Relations, Agent Workbench, Content, Knowledge, Einstellungen) and, beside them, the signed-in partner's own open CRM tasks sorted by urgency (overdue, today, this week, later, no due date), with filter chips, a link to the record and "Erledigt" with a one-step undo. Opens at once from a copy kept in this browser. Writes only the completion of a task. On a phone the tiles come first, then the list. | Attio (`whoami`, `list-tasks`, `update-task`) |
| `deal-cockpit.html` | Deal Cockpit | Four tabs. **Deals:** the deal list ranked by score x urgency, with the three scores (Quality, Thesis Fit, Urgency), a scorecard drawer, and a "Score this deal" action; moves a deal to another stage and writes a note, each after a confirmation; the tab shows the size of the running pipeline (open stages only, `CONFIG.pipelineStages`). **Aufgaben:** the open CRM tasks per deal, added and completed from the drawer. **Inbox** and **Autopilot:** the deal domain's approvals, the autopilot switch and the feed with undo, in the page itself on its own store (`db`), with the same checks as the Agent Workbench, including the Kontext block. The deal list opens at once from a copy kept in this browser and refreshes from the connector in the background. On a phone the side menu sits behind a burger. | Attio (read the deal list, write the six score fields, stage, notes, tasks); Google Drive (read the fund's thesis, criteria and scoring matrix; Kontext documents); Gmail (drafts only, `get_thread` for the Kontext block); Claude `sample` for scoring; artifact `db` |
| `investors.html` | Investor Relations | The LP pipeline in five tabs. **Liste:** a table with LP fit tiers and a next action per investor. **Board:** one column per status (`CONFIG.boardColumns`), one compact card per investor with LP fit chip, ticket and days since the last contact; a card is moved to another status by drag and drop (mouse, touch after a short hold, or the menu on the card), written after the move with a toast and one-step undo, and a move into the hard or closed status asks for an inline confirmation first; every column shows the sum of the tickets (`CONFIG.fields.ticket`, the average ticket size, never a commitment) and every KPI tile does too; contact older than 14 days is marked in red inside the running pipeline (never for the passive status). **Aufgaben:** the open tasks per investor, added and completed. **Inbox** and **Autopilot:** the investors' approvals, the autopilot switch and the feed with undo, in the page itself on its own store (`db`), as in the Deal Cockpit. Changes an investor's status, writes a note and adds or completes a task, each after a confirmation. Full-screen drawer on a phone. Opens at once from a copy kept in this browser and refreshes in the background. | Attio (read the list, write the status, notes, tasks); Gmail (drafts only, `get_thread`); Google Drive (`search_files`, for the Kontext block); artifact `db` |
| `inbox.html` | Agent Workbench | The agents' desk, formerly called Inbox (the file keeps its name). Tabs: **Aktivität** (first, read-only: every run and every action of all five modules, newest first, with day separators, filter chips per module and kind - Läufe, Aktionen, Fehler -, the target linked to its record when known, mode and counts, and a drawer with the stored fields), **Freigaben** (approvals: mails, deal stage, investor status, newsletter, task, note, with a drawer on the phone and a record filter `#record=<id>`), **Eingang** (Gmail intake), **Autopilot** (the switch per module that keeps its data in this store: newsletter and notes; the dealflow and investors switches moved to their own pages and show a pointer card) and **Protokoll** (the audit feed with undo). Under every proposal a closed **Kontext** block loads on first open, from the viewer's own connectors and without storing anything: the mail thread, the Drive documents whose title contains the deal name, and the CRM record. Dealflow and investors write their runs and audit entries into this store too (`mirror-plan`), so Aktivität shows all agents; those mirrored entries are a read-only log here (no Rückgängig, not counted in the badge). | Attio, Gmail (drafts only, never sends), Google Drive (search only), artifact `db` |
| `knowledge.html` | Knowledge | Lists the documents in the fund's Drive knowledge folder, shows which scoring keys are the fund's own document and which fall back to the fund-neutral template, and renders any document. | Google Drive |
| `profile.html` | Profil | Who is signed in, which connectors this view reaches, the tool chosen per role and per module (dealflow, investors, newsletter, shared) with its live connection status, the alternatives and, as text, when the module's routine fires (`CONFIG.routineSchedule`), what the agents may do without approval, and **Content-Kanäle**: the publishing channels (LinkedIn, website, WhatsApp, newsletter, link) kept in the Drive file `CONFIG.channelsFile`, created, changed and switched off here, never deleted. | Attio, Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, Granola (one harmless read tool each; Drive also creates and renames the channels file), artifact `db`, user |
| `newsletter.html` | Content | Three tabs. **Newsletter:** the archive of issues (draft, approved, sent), an editor with a live preview, the fund's templates read from the Drive knowledge folder (`newsletter-template*.html`, with preview thumbnails) and the hand-over as a Gmail draft to the partners. **Artikel:** articles and whitepaper drafts as files in the knowledge folder (`article-<slug>.md`), listed together with the newsletters in one shared list (`pr-index.json`). **Template:** every `newsletter-template*.html` in the folder (one that cannot be used says why) with an editor that saves a new file; the old one stays. Every item has **Veröffentlichen**: a text proposed per channel (`sample`), then the channel's own share dialog (LinkedIn, WhatsApp), a Gmail draft, a copied text with the stored link, or a website request that a session of the website repository turns into a draft pull request (`tools/ops/content-cli.mjs`). Nothing is sent or posted by the page. Issues are kept in the artifact's own store (`db`); the list and the channels live in the Drive folder. | Google Drive (search, download, create, rename), Gmail (drafts only), Claude `sample`, artifact `db` |

**The browser copy of the lists.** Deal Cockpit, Investor Relations and Start keep the last list they read from the connector in the browser's local storage of the artifact (keys `fundos.cockpit.deals`, `fundos.investors.list` and `fundos.start.tasks`), so the next open shows it at once with the pill "Stand von vor n Min · aktualisiere …" (marked "veraltet" after seven days) while the connector refreshes it. Only connector data is stored; it never enters the artifact's shared store, a repository or another device. Every storage access is guarded: a private window, a full quota or blocked site data make the page load as without a cache. The diagnostics area offers "Zwischenspeicher leeren"; the Profil screen names the behaviour.

**Keeping your fund out of the repository.** `bash tools/check-screen-templates.sh` scans every template for values that must never be committed: Claude artifact links, uuids, calendar booking links, Drive-style ids (a run of 25 or more letters, digits, `_` and `-` with at least one of each), Drive links and e-mail addresses outside `.example` / `example.com`. Add your fund's own names, slugs and ids to a **local denylist**: either the git-ignored file `tools/.fund-denylist` or any file named by `FUND_OS_DENYLIST`, one extended regular expression per line, matched case-insensitively. Never commit it. The check greps every template with it, so a value that slips from your `CONFIG` into the shared code is caught before you publish.

The page text is German, as in the source pages; change the strings if your partners prefer another language. Comments inside the pure code regions still point at the source project's specs; they are explanations, not links you need.

## How to fill CONFIG

Open the page's script; the first thing in it is the `CONFIG` block, with a comment on every key. Edit nothing else. The keys the pages share:

| Key | What to put | Where to find it |
|---|---|---|
| `fundName` | The name shown in the side menu | Your `masterData.fundName` |
| `attioWorkspace` | The slug in your Attio URLs | `https://app.attio.com/<slug>/...` |
| `dealList`, `investorList` | The list slugs (Investor Relations needs `dealList` too, for the stage proposals of its Inbox tab) | `crmFields.dealList`, `crmFields.investorList` in `~/.fund-os/user-config.json`; or the Attio list settings |
| `fields.*`, `dealStageField`, `dealSourceField`, `investorStatusField` | List-entry attribute slugs | `crmFields.*`; or Attio, list settings, attribute, API slug |
| `stages`, `statuses`, `liveStages`, `passedStages`, `pipelineStages`, `forbiddenStages`, `forbiddenStatuses`, `boardColumns`, ... | Stage and status titles, in pipeline order | The Attio status options of your lists. They must match **exactly**; a stage that is not in the list is shown but cannot be chosen |
| `companiesObjectId` (Agent Workbench, Deal Cockpit, Investor Relations) | The id of the Attio `companies` object | Ask the Attio connector once for the `companies` object and copy its id |
| `driveFolderId`, `driveFolderName` | The Drive folder with the fund's thesis, criteria, scoring matrix, newsletter templates, content list and channels file, and its name for the connection line | The id at the end of the folder URL. Name the files `investment-thesis`, `evaluation-criteria`, `startup-scoring-matrix`, `newsletter-template.html`, ... |
| `fundDescription`, `scoringLabel` (Deal Cockpit) | One clause for the scoring prompt, and the footer line of a written scorecard | Your own words, e.g. "a pre-seed/seed fund in <region> for <sector> software" |
| `scoreFields`, `investorFitFields`, `archivedFields`, `routineSchedule`, `locale` (Profil) | Display-only lists for the rules table, when each module's routine fires as free text for the tools table (`dealflow`, `investors`, `newsletter`, `shared`; empty = not shown), and the default language and time zone | `crmFields`, and the schedules of the Routines if you run the autopilot as Routines |
| `channelsFile` (Content, Profil) | The title of the Drive file in the knowledge folder that holds the publishing channels; both pages must carry the same value. Default `content-channels.json`. The content list is the fixed file `pr-index.json` in the same folder. While the file is missing, five neutral seed channels apply (LinkedIn company, LinkedIn personal, website, WhatsApp community, newsletter); the first save in the Profil page creates the file | Leave the default unless the folder already holds a file by another name |
| `newsletterFooter` (Content) | The sender line in the footer of an issue and of its text version; `fundName` also names the header band of the built-in template | Your own words, e.g. the fund's name |
| `intakeStage`, `intakeSource`, `ackReplyDays`, `gmailQuery` (Agent Workbench) | The stage and source a mail taken from Gmail gets, the working days promised in the acknowledgement draft, the default Gmail search | Your own pipeline |
| `links` | The seven side-menu URLs: `start`, `deals`, `investors`, `inbox` (the Agent Workbench), `knowledge`, `newsletter` (Content), `profile` | See the next section; leave empty until every screen is published |

The stage and status names the examples ship with are common ones, not a recommendation. If a key is left empty the page loads but the Attio call for it fails with a visible error in the Diagnose panel.

## Order to publish

The side menu links the seven screens to each other, and each URL exists only after its screen has been published once. So:

1. Fill `CONFIG` in all seven pages **except** `links`.
2. Publish all seven as new artifacts, each with its capabilities from the table above. Note the seven URLs.
3. Paste the seven URLs into `CONFIG.links` of **every** page (start, deals, investors, inbox, knowledge, newsletter, profile), then republish each page to its own URL (read the artifact first, then update it).
4. Open the Profil screen and press "Neu prüfen" on each connector; approve the connector permissions once per partner.

The Start entry is the first item of every side menu, above the areas, and the Start page shows one tile per area from the same URLs. An empty link renders as a disabled entry marked "folgt" in the Profil screen, a dead link in the side menu and a disabled tile on Start; nothing breaks.

Once the pages run, the fund's own values that must not sit in a repository (a task assignee, the LP deck link, the booking link) belong in the file `fund-settings.json` in the Drive knowledge folder, which the autopilot skills overlay on the configuration for one run (`deal-score-cli.mjs fund-settings`, see `tools/ops/README.md`); the pages do not read it.

## What must not drift

Keep the pure code regions identical to this folder's copies when you edit: `parseAttioText` (Attio answers in a YAML-like text, not JSON), the scoring mirror (`// ==== scoring-mirror:begin`, the same arithmetic as `tools/ops/lib/scoring.mjs`), the knowledge and scorecard regions, the rank region (`// region:rank`, mirrored byte for byte in `tools/ops/digest-cli.mjs`), the task region (`// region:tasks`, the same in the Deal Cockpit and Investor Relations), the local-cache region (`// region:localcache`, the same in the Deal Cockpit, Investor Relations and Start), the context region (`// region:context`, the same in the Agent Workbench, the Deal Cockpit and Investor Relations), the Inbox `guards` region and the `autopilot` region (the same in those three; the autopilot regions differ in their one `MODULES` line), the `store-tabs` region (the Inbox and Autopilot tabs of one domain on its own store, the same in the Deal Cockpit and Investor Relations), the `permhelp` region (what a page says and offers when `use('mcp')` is null; the same in all seven pages), the `channels` region (the publishing channels, their file, share URLs and the length cut; the same in Content and Profil), the Content render grammar (`// ==== newsletter-render:begin`) and the Profil `tools` region. A few lines inside them read `CONFIG` instead of a literal (the folder id, the fund line of the prompt, the footer label, the six score slugs, the rank region's field slugs and rejected stage, the forbidden stages and statuses, the channels file and fund name, the newsletter footer and header band); everything else is the source text, unchanged.

`bash tools/check-screen-templates.sh` checks the folder:

- each page: the script parses (`node --check`), `CONFIG` is declared exactly once, and the page names no fund;
- the scoring mirror against `tools/ops/lib/scoring.mjs` (`tools/check-scoring-mirror.mjs`);
- the rank region against `tools/ops/digest-cli.mjs`, and both on the digest fixtures (`tools/check-digest-mirror.mjs`);
- the shared regions byte-identical across their pages (`guards`, `autopilot` but for its `MODULES` line, `context`, `store-tabs`, `tasks`, `localcache`, `permhelp`, `channels`; the pages are named in the header of `tools/check-screen-templates.sh`), each with a negative control: a mutated copy must be reported: fix a copy, fix them all;
- the Start entry first in the side menu of every page, and `links.start` in every `CONFIG`;
- that exactly these seven pages are in the folder.
