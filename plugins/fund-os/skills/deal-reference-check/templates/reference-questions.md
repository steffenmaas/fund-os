# Reference Check Guide

> **How to use:** Use this guide for structured founder and customer reference calls. The `deal-reference-check` skill loads it automatically if saved as `reference-questions` in your Drive folder. Aim for 3–5 professional references and 2–3 customer references per deal.

---

## Before the call

- Confirm their relationship to the founder (colleague, manager, investor, customer)
- Set context: "We're considering an investment in [Company]. This is a confidential call — I'd appreciate your honest perspective."
- Tell them you'll take notes and share a summary with your IC (not with the founder)
- Budget 20–30 minutes

---

## Professional reference questions (for colleagues, former managers, investors)

### Capability
1. In what context did you work with [Founder]? How long and how closely?
2. What are [Founder]'s strongest skills? Where do you see them excel?
3. What are their genuine development areas — the things they know they need to work on?

### Execution
4. Give me an example of a time they had to solve a hard problem with limited resources. What happened?
5. How do they handle pressure, setbacks or unexpected bad news?
6. How do they make decisions when data is incomplete or the team disagrees?

### Team and culture
7. How does [Founder] hire? Who do they bring in around them?
8. How do they give feedback to the team — and receive it?
9. Would you invest in them / work with them again? Why or why not?

### Specific to this company
10. Does the problem they're solving match what you'd expect from them, given their background?
11. Is there anything about their trajectory or this specific venture that gives you pause?

---

## Customer reference questions (for current or former customers)

### Problem and fit
1. What problem were you trying to solve when you found [Company]?
2. Why did you choose them over alternatives?
3. How long have you been a customer and how has usage changed?

### Value
4. What's the clearest way [Company]'s product has made a difference for your team?
5. If [Company] shut down tomorrow, what would you do? How hard would it be to replace?

### Relationship
6. How do you find the team to work with — responsiveness, execution, listening to feedback?
7. Have there been any significant issues? How were they handled?

### Forward-looking
8. Are you expanding your use of the product, maintaining it, or have you had any conversations about changing?
9. Would you recommend [Company] to a peer in a similar role? Have you already?

---

## Scoring guide

After each call, rate the reference on a 1–5 scale:

| Score | Signal |
|---|---|
| 5 | Strong positive — would hire/buy again without hesitation, specific examples |
| 4 | Positive — genuine enthusiasm, minor caveats noted |
| 3 | Neutral — polite, no red flags, no real conviction either way |
| 2 | Cautious — notable hesitation, vague answers, redirected questions |
| 1 | Negative — explicit concern, recommended caution, or refused to answer key questions |

**Threshold:** Do not proceed to IC with fewer than 3 references averaging ≥4, or any reference scoring 1–2 on a core question.
