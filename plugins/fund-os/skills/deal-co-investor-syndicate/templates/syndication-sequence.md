# Co-Investor Deal Share — 3-Step Sequence

> **How to use:** Use this sequence when sharing a deal with potential co-investors. The goal is to give them enough to get excited, not enough to go around you. Edit [bracketed] fields. Save as `syndication-sequence` in Drive for the skill to use it automatically.

---

## Step 1 — Deal intro (send after IC approval to proceed)

**Subject:** Deal share: [Company] · [Round] · [1-line thesis fit]

---

Hi [Name],

Sharing a deal I thought might fit your mandate — [Company], [one sentence description].

**The numbers:** [ARR or revenue], [growth rate], [NRR if SaaS], [runway].
**The team:** [Founder background in one sentence — what makes them credible].
**Why now:** [One sentence on timing / market shift].
**Round:** [Size], [pre-money], [lead / co-lead status].

We're [leading / co-leading] at [our cheque size]. There's [allocation available / limited space — be specific].

Happy to share the deck and intro you to the founders if this resonates. Let me know by [date] if you'd like more.

[Signature]

---

## Step 2 — Follow-up with materials (send 4–5 days later if interested or no reply)

**Subject:** Re: [Company] — deck + intro available

---

Hi [Name],

Following up on [Company] — wanted to make sure this didn't get buried.

[One sentence update if any: a new metric, a reference you completed, a term sheet development.]

I've attached the deck. If you want a direct intro to [Founder name], happy to make that happen this week.

We're looking to close the round by [date]. Let me know if you'd like to move forward or if the fit isn't right.

[Signature]

---

## Step 3 — Close the loop (send 5–7 days before round close)

**Subject:** Re: [Company] — closing [date]

---

Hi [Name],

Quick heads up — we're closing [Company]'s round on [date]. There's [€X] of allocation remaining.

If you'd like to participate, I'll need a soft commitment by [date - 3 days] to hold space. Happy to jump on a call today or tomorrow if you have questions.

If it's not the right fit, no worries — I'll keep you in mind for the next one.

[Signature]

---

## Guidance notes

- Step 1 should create curiosity, not answer every question. If they ask nothing, the note was too long.
- Never share the full data room in Step 1. The deck comes in Step 2 after they express interest.
- Be explicit about allocation and deadline — scarcity is real, signal it honestly.
- If a co-investor consistently ghosts, remove them from the list. Quality over quantity.
- For strategic co-investors, personalise Step 1 with 1–2 sentences on why this deal fits their specific portfolio.
