# O1 Startup Scoring Matrix
# Ocean One Ventures Fund I — Internal Reference
# Version: 1.0 | Last updated: 2026-06-26

---

## Overview

The O1 Startup Scoring model scores early-stage maritime-leisure SaaS startups across 10 weighted dimensions. Total score is /100.

**Scoring methodology — CRITICAL RULES:**

1. **Score ALL 10 dimensions on actual evidence, always.** Never zero out dimensions because of thesis mismatches (wrong geography, stage, model). Hard-pass filters belong ONLY in the O1 Thesis Fit star rating and Recommended action.
2. **Total score = exact arithmetic sum of all 10 dimension numerators.** Do NOT set it manually. Compute it AFTER scoring all dimensions. Example: if numerators are 14+12+13+9+8+9+8+9+4+5 = 91, then total = 91.
3. **Technology & Product weight is /10 — ALWAYS.** Never write /15 for this dimension.
4. **Score bands reflect company quality on the merits.** A company can score Strong Buy (90+) and still receive a ❌ Pass in Recommended action if it fails O1 thesis filters (e.g. a US Series B marina-booking platform can score 91/100 on the merits and still be a Hard Pass for a European pre-seed Fund I).

**Dimension max scores (sum to 100):**
- Team & Founder-Market Fit: /20
- Market Opportunity: /15
- Problem–Solution Fit: /15
- Technology & Product: /10  ← ALWAYS /10, never /15
- Business Model: /10
- Traction & Validation: /10
- Competition & Differentiation: /10
- Go-to-Market Strategy: /10
- Financial Planning & Use of Funds: /5
- Exit Potential: /5
- **TOTAL: /100**

## Screening Depth Labels

Use the label that matches how much information is available:

- **First screening** — Name + website/LinkedIn only. Score mainly on sector + geography + archetype fit. Expect 4–6 dimensions as "No information available."
- **Pitch deck screening** — Full deck reviewed. Most dimensions scoreable. Traction and financial figures from deck slides.
- **Due diligence screening** — Full data room, references, founder calls. All dimensions must be scored.

---

## Zero-Information Rule

If a dimension cannot be assessed given available data: score = 0, label = "No information available." Do not guess or infer. This applies mainly at First screening depth.

## 10 Dimensions — Weights & Scoring Signals

### 1. Team & Founder-Market Fit — Weight: 20%

The single most important dimension. Score for maritime domain depth + technical/commercial balance + prior exits or operator experience.

| Score | Signal |
|---|---|
| 90–100 | Serial founder with prior maritime exit + technical co-founder; full-time; deep operator network |
| 70–89 | Strong domain expertise (5+ yrs in sector) + complementary co-founder; full-time |
| 50–69 | One strong founder, team incomplete; or domain expertise without tech depth |
| 30–49 | Generalist team, no maritime background; learning the sector |
| 0–29 | Solo founder or team with no relevant experience; part-time |
| 0 | No information available |

**Bonus signals:** Prior Gartner recognition, accelerator alumni (YC, Techstars, O1), advisory board with maritime operators.

---

### 2. Market Opportunity — Weight: 15%

Score for SAM size, growth rate, and bottom-up validation. We need SAM ≥€500M serviceable within 5 years.

| Score | Signal |
|---|---|
| 90–100 | SAM >€1B; bottom-up validated; >8% CAGR; maritime leisure sub-segment clearly defined |
| 70–89 | SAM €500M–1B; reasonable bottom-up; sector tailwinds visible |
| 50–69 | TAM large but SAM unclear; or niche segment with limited growth evidence |
| 30–49 | Small or declining market; geography-limited; <€200M SAM |
| 0–29 | No credible market sizing; consumer-only; outside maritime leisure |
| 0 | No information available |

**Reference:** O1 Maritime LEISURE software TAM = €7.5–10.9B (2030F); core SAM = €1.5–2.5B.

---

### 3. Problem–Solution Fit — Weight: 15%

Score for how acutely the problem is felt by operators and how precisely the solution addresses it. Mission-critical = highest scores.

| Score | Signal |
|---|---|
| 90–100 | Mission-critical (no software = no operations); strong operator validation (LOIs, paid pilots, advisory); clear before/after |
| 70–89 | Important but not mission-critical; multiple customers confirm pain; clear ROI metric |
| 50–69 | Problem real but nice-to-have; limited customer validation; ROI unclear |
| 30–49 | Problem exists but solution is weak fit or easily substituted |
| 0–29 | Solution looking for problem; no evidence operators care |
| 0 | No information available |

---

### 4. Technology & Product — Weight: 10% ← ALWAYS /10, never /15

Score for product maturity, defensibility, and AI/data moat potential.

| Score | Signal |
|---|---|
| 90–100 | Live product with paying customers; proprietary data moat or AI layer; defensible IP; integration-first architecture |
| 70–89 | MVP in pilots; clear differentiation vs. spreadsheets/generic tools; roadmap solid |

| 30–49 | Concept only; no live product; heavy reliance on third-party tools with no moat |
| 0–29 | No product; pure idea stage with no technical progress |
| 0 | No information available |

### 5. Business Model — Weight: 10%

Score for SaaS quality: recurring revenue, gross margin potential, pricing logic, asset-light model.

| Score | Signal |
|---|---|

| 70–89 | SaaS with services component (<30%); ACV €2K–5K; GM 60–75%; clear path to pure SaaS |
| 50–69 | Mixed model (SaaS + hardware or transactional); GM 40–60%; pricing not yet proven |
| 30–49 | Heavy services; low-margin; unclear path to SaaS multiples |
| 0–29 | No recurring revenue model; one-off or project-based; asset-heavy |
| 0 | No information available |

### 7. Competition & Differentiation — Weight: 10%

| Score | Signal |

| 0 | No information available |

| Score | Signal |

### 10. Exit Potential — Weight: 5%

Score for acquirer landscape and realistic exit path at 3–4x MOIC on €250K–€1M ticket.

| Score | Signal |
|---|---|
| 90–100 | Multiple credible strategic acquirers identified; comparable exits (FareHarbor, Mews, Cloudbeds); clear 5–7yr path to €50–250M exit |
| 70–89 | 2–3 plausible acquirers; comparable M&A activity in sector; exit logic coherent |
| 50–69 | Exit path imaginable but acquirers thin or not yet active in maritime leisure software |
| 30–49 | Exit logic unclear; no comparable transactions |
| 0–29 | No exit path; niche too small or acquirers absent |
| 0 | No information available |

## Secondary Ratings

These are appended after the dimension breakdown. They do not affect the /100 score.

### O1 Thesis Fit — ★/5

How well does this company fit the Ocean One investment thesis?

| Stars | Criteria |
|---|---|
| ★★★★★ | Maritime LEISURE B2B SaaS · DACH/EU geography · Pre-Seed/Seed · Asset-light · Recurring revenue · Operator-facing |
| ★★★★☆ | Meets 5/6 criteria; minor gap (e.g. slightly off-stage or geography adjacent) |
| ★★★☆☆ | Meets 3–4 criteria; meaningful thesis stretch required |
| ★★☆☆☆ | Meets 1–2 criteria; significant stretch from core thesis |
| ★☆☆☆☆ | Maritime adjacent but far from core thesis |

### Impact Fit — ★/5

Does this company create measurable positive impact on the maritime leisure ecosystem and ocean sustainability?

| Stars | Criteria |
|---|---|
| ★★★★★ | Directly reduces maritime environmental footprint OR significantly improves economic inclusion/access to maritime leisure |
| ★★★★☆ | Clear secondary impact (digitises fragmented industry, enables compliance, improves safety, reduces waste) |
| ★★★☆☆ | Neutral to positive impact; efficiency gains without direct environmental angle |
| ★★☆☆☆ | Indirect/minor impact; no sustainability angle |
| ★☆☆☆☆ | No impact dimension |

## What Ocean One Is NOT

These categories are outside the O1 thesis for Fund I. When a company matches one of these, note it explicitly in the **O1 Thesis Fit** star rating reason AND the **Recommended action**. Score all 10 dimensions on the merits anyway — the score reflects company quality regardless of thesis fit.

Outside O1 Fund I thesis:
- Commercial shipping / freight / port logistics
- Naval / defence
- Offshore energy
- Consumer apps without B2B maritime angle
- Hardware-only (no SaaS layer)
- APAC-focused
- Pre-idea / academic research stage

**Important:** A company outside thesis CAN still receive a high O1 score (e.g. 91/100) if it is excellent. The Recommended action will say ❌ Pass with the thesis mismatch as the reason. The score is still logged in Attio because it provides signal about company quality independently of Fund I fit.

## Common Errors to Avoid

These bugs have occurred in past scorings and must not recur:

1. **Zero-out bug** — Zeroing all 10 dimension scores when a hard-pass thesis filter fails (wrong geo, wrong stage, acquired). WRONG. Always score all 10 dimensions on the evidence. Only the O1 Thesis Fit stars and Recommended action reflect thesis filters.

2. **Technology /15 bug** — Writing Technology & Product as `/15` in the scorecard. WRONG. It is always `/10`. The old "10% (15% in due diligence)" note in an earlier version of this file caused 13 erroneous evaluations in Attio, which had to be corrected in bulk.

3. **Manual total** — Setting the total score to a "nice" number before or instead of summing dimension numerators. WRONG. Total = exact arithmetic sum of all 10 numerators, computed last.

4. **Wrong Attio slug** — Using `ai_investment_score` as the field slug. WRONG. `ai_investment_score` is the archived/old slug. Correct slug: `o1_investment_score`.

5. **Wrong Attio write method** — Using `update-list-entry-by-record-id` or `update-record`. WRONG. Always use `update-list-entry-by-id` at the list-entry level.
