# Due Diligence Framework

> **How to use:** Edit this framework to match your fund's DD process. Save it as `dd-framework` in your Drive knowledge folder — the investment memo, IC pack and reference check skills load it automatically.

## DD workstreams and owners

| Workstream | Owner | Inputs | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thesis & market | Partner | Deck, market data | Market map + TAM validation |
| Team | Partner | LinkedIn, references | Reference check summary |
| Product | Associate | Demo, tech review | Product assessment |
| Financials | Associate | Data room, model | Financial model + benchmarks |
| Legal | Partner / counsel | Cap table, contracts | Legal red flags |
| Impact (if applicable) | Associate | Impact data | Impact assessment |

## Standard DD timeline

| Day | Milestone |
|---|---|
| 0 | IC approval to proceed to DD |
| 1–3 | Data room access; kick off all workstreams |
| 3–7 | Founder deep-dives (product, market, financials) |
| 5–10 | Reference calls (3–5 founder references, 2–3 customer references) |
| 7–14 | Market map and competitor analysis completed |
| 10–14 | Financial model built and stress-tested |
| 14–21 | IC memo first draft circulated |
| 21–28 | IC decision |

## Data room checklist

**Company**
- [ ] Certificate of incorporation and cap table (fully diluted)
- [ ] Shareholder and investor agreements
- [ ] Option pool details and vesting schedules

**Financials**
- [ ] 3-year P&L actuals (monthly)
- [ ] Balance sheet and cash flow statement
- [ ] MRR/ARR waterfall by cohort
- [ ] Unit economics dashboard (CAC, LTV, payback)
- [ ] 3-year financial model with assumptions

**Product & tech**
- [ ] Product roadmap (6–12 months)
- [ ] Architecture overview or technical summary
- [ ] IP ownership confirmation

**Commercial**
- [ ] Customer list (top 10 by revenue, anonymised if needed)
- [ ] Pipeline and sales process overview
- [ ] Key customer contracts (2–3 examples)

**Team**
- [ ] Org chart and team bios
- [ ] Employment agreements (key hires)
- [ ] Reference contacts (3–5 founders + 2–3 customers)

## IC memo requirements

The investment memo must address:
1. Investment thesis — why this, why now, why this team
2. Market — TAM/SAM/SOM with bottom-up validation
3. Product — differentiation and moat
4. Business model — unit economics and path to profitability
5. Traction — key metrics benchmarked against peers
6. Team — founder-market fit and reference signal
7. Financials — 3-year model and valuation
8. Risks — top 3 risks and mitigants
9. Deal terms — round structure, our participation, ownership
10. Open questions — unresolved items before IC decision
