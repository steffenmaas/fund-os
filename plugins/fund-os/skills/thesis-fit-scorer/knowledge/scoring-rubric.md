# Thesis Fit Scoring Rubric

> **How to use:** Edit the weights and thresholds to match your fund's investment thesis. The `thesis-fit-scorer` skill loads this file at runtime — no re-deploy needed.

## Dimensions and weights

| Dimension | Weight | What to assess |
|---|---|---|
| Team | 20% | Domain expertise, prior exits, team completeness, founder-market fit |
| Market | 20% | TAM/SAM/SOM, growth rate, timing, regulatory tailwind |
| Problem-Solution | 15% | Problem severity, solution clarity, differentiation |
| Technology / Moat | 15% | Tech differentiation, defensibility, IP |
| Traction | 15% | Revenue, growth, customer quality, retention signals |
| Business model | 10% | Unit economics path, pricing clarity, scalability |
| Exit potential | 5% | Comparable exits, strategic buyers, IPO path |

## Scoring scale

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 9–10 | Exceptional — best-in-class evidence |
| 7–8 | Strong — clear evidence, minor gaps |
| 5–6 | Adequate — present but underdeveloped |
| 3–4 | Weak — significant gaps or red flags |
| 1–2 | Very weak — missing or contradictory |
| 0 | Absent — no evidence, mark INSUFFICIENT DATA |

## Verdict thresholds

| Score | Verdict |
|---|---|
| 80–100 | **PROCEED** — advance to DD |
| 60–79 | **WATCHLIST** — follow up in [X] months or wait for milestone |
| < 60 | **PASS** — decline with specific feedback |
