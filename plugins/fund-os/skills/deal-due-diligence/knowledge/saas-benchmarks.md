# SaaS Benchmarks for IC Memo Validation

> Used by `deal-due-diligence` when validating the Traction and Valuation sections.

Use these when writing the memo's Valuation and Traction sections:

| Metric | Healthy | Best-in-Class | Benchmark Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| ARR growth (<€5M ARR) | >50% YoY | >70% YoY | SaaS Capital 2025 |
| NRR | >104% | >115% | OpenView 2025 |
| Gross margin | 70–75% | 80%+ | Bessemer 2025 |
| LTV:CAC | >3:1 | 5–7:1 | SaaS Capital 2025 |
| CAC payback (SMB) | <12 months | <6 months | Gainsight 2025 |
| EV/ARR (pre-seed SaaS) | 8–15x | 20x+ | Dealroom Q1 2026 |

Mark any metric below benchmark as `[BELOW BENCHMARK: ...]` in the memo.
