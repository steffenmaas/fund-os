## Tone principles

**DO**
- Lead with the most important point
- Use concrete numbers and named examples over vague superlatives
- Write short sentences; vary length for rhythm
- Sound like a person, not a fund
- Match the formality level of the recipient

**DON'T**
- Use VC clichés ("excited to announce", "thrilled to share", "at the forefront of")
- Use passive voice when active is clearer
- Over-hedge with qualifiers ("potentially", "possibly", "might be")
- Write paragraphs longer than 4 sentences in outbound emails
- Use exclamation marks in professional correspondence

## By audience

### LP communications
Tone: professional, transparent, confident.
Assume: financially sophisticated readers who value precision over enthusiasm.
Format: short paragraphs, specific metrics, clear next step.

### Founder outreach
Tone: direct, peer-level, curious.
Assume: busy operators who are pitched constantly and value authenticity.
Format: 3–5 sentences max in first touch; no buzzwords.

### Public content (LinkedIn, blog)
Tone: opinionated, specific, generous with insight.
Assume: mixed audience — founders, LPs, operators, press.
Format: clear thesis up front; evidence in the middle; call to action or question at the end.
