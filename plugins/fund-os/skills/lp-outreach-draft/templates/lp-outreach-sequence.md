# LP Outreach — 3-Step Email Sequence

> **How to use:** This is a best-practice template for cold and warm LP outreach. Edit the [bracketed] fields. The three-step sequence follows the principle: introduce → add value → close the loop. Save as `lp-outreach-sequence` in your Drive if you want the skill to use it automatically.

---

## Step 1 — First touch (send on a Tuesday or Wednesday morning)

**Subject:** [Fund Name] · [1 specific hook: fund thesis or mutual connection]

---

Hi [First name],

[One sentence on why you're reaching out to them specifically — reference their mandate, a recent commitment, or a mutual connection. Not generic.]

[Fund Name] is a [size] fund focused on [2–3 word thesis]. We're currently [raising Fund I / deploying Fund II — be specific]. [One sentence on what makes the fund distinct — a conviction, a network, a track record.]

[If warm intro:] [Mutual contact] suggested I reach out — [one sentence on why they thought you'd be a fit for each other.]

Would a 20-minute call in the next few weeks make sense? I'm happy to send over our teaser in advance.

[Signature]

---

## Step 2 — Follow-up (send 6–8 days later if no reply)

**Subject:** Re: [Fund Name] · [same subject as Step 1]

---

Hi [First name],

Following up briefly on my note from last week.

[One sentence of new information or value: a recent portfolio milestone, a press mention, a data point relevant to their mandate.]

Happy to keep this short — even a 15-minute intro call would be useful to understand whether there's a fit.

[Signature]

---

## Step 3 — Final bump (send 7–10 days after Step 2 if no reply)

**Subject:** Re: [Fund Name] · [same subject]

---

Hi [First name],

I'll keep this brief — one last note before I stop cluttering your inbox.

If the timing isn't right or [Fund Name] isn't a fit for your portfolio, I completely understand. Happy to reconnect at a different stage of the fund.

If there is any interest, I'd welcome a short call. Either way, I've attached our one-pager for your records.

Thanks for your time.

[Signature]

---

## Guidance notes

- **Step 1** should be ≤150 words. Any longer and open rates drop.
- **Step 2** adds new information — don't just say "following up on my last email."
- **Step 3** explicitly gives them permission to say no. This increases reply rates.
- Never send all three steps in the same week.
- Personalise Step 1 before sending. Generic first touches are worse than no outreach.
- Track open rates by subject line. Specific subjects outperform vague ones.
